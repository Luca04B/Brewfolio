# Brewfolio

Brewfolio is the product and repository being built as a learning-focused portfolio application. This glossary will grow only when business terms are agreed during feature design.

## Language

**Brewfolio**:
The product represented by this monorepo.
_Avoid_: the app, the thing

**Coffee Bean**:
A specific coffee product that a user can keep in their current collection and use for recipes and brews.
_Avoid_: Coffee, Bean, Coffee Beans when referring to one item

**Recipe**:
A reusable plan for preparing coffee, including the intended brewing method, quantities, temperature, grind, and target brew time.
_Avoid_: Brew, Coffee Brew

**Brewing Method**:
A configurable way of preparing coffee that can be selected by Recipes and deactivated without removing historical references.
_Avoid_: Recipe, Preparation Type

**Coffee Brew**:
A record of one completed coffee preparation using a Coffee Bean and a Recipe at a particular time, optionally including a rating and notes.
_Avoid_: Coffee, Recipe, Brew Log
